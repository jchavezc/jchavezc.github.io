Course Overview

Math 142 continues the study of calculus where Math 141 left off with the integral.

By the end of the semester, students will:

  • Demonstrate proficiency with the fundamental tools and techniques of integration, infinite sequences and series, and differential equations.
  • Use integration to solve a variety of applied problems.
  • Identify connections between math and other branches of science and engineering.
  • Express mathematical ideas and arguments more formally and more clearly.
  • Understand some of the basic numerical methods to compute derivatives.

Prerequisites

  • C- or better in MTH 141.

Textbooks

  • Calculus: Single Variable by Hughes-Hallett et al., 7th Ed.

  • Midterm Exam 2: 15%
