--- layout: course title: Calculus II description: This course continues the study of Calculus. instructor: Prof. Chávez Casillas #year: term: Summer Only location: Online #time: Mondays and Wednesdays, 2:00-3:30 PM course_id: MTH142 ## Course Overview Math 142 continues the study of calculus where Math 141 left off with the integral. By the end of the semester, students will: - Demonstrate proficiency with the fundamental tools and techniques of integration, infinite sequences and series, and differential equations. - Use integration to solve a variety of applied problems. - Identify connections between math and other branches of science and engineering. - Express mathematical ideas and arguments more formally and more clearly. - Understand some of the basic numerical methods to compute derivatives. ## Prerequisites - C- or better in MTH 141. ## Textbooks - Calculus: Single Variable by Hughes-Hallett et al., 7th Ed. ## Grading - Midterm Exam 1: 15% - Midterm Exam 2: 15% - Homework: 30% - Cumulative Final Exam: 40%
